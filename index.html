Tuner-G

Precision Tuning for iPhone & iPad

A fast, accurate chromatic tuner for guitar, bass, ukulele, violin and voice. Clear feedback on a screen that stays still while you tune, free to use, with an optional one-time Pro unlock.

Features

Accurate Pitch Detection

Choose how notes are detected: YIN for most instruments, pYIN for voice and noisy rooms, or a low-power mode for long sessions, rebuilt for accuracy in 2.0.

Instruments & Tunings

6- and 7-string guitar, 4- and 5-string bass, ukulele, violin and a chromatic mode for voice. Standard, drop and open tunings, a custom A4 and a strict mode for tuning string by string.

Four App Skins NEW

Classic, Spectral Atelier, Studio Ledger and Vector Air: four complete looks for the tuner and every screen around it. All free.

Tuning History NEW

A log of each string you bring in tune, kept on your device. Turn recording off or clear it whenever you like. Free.

Chord Library NEW

Guitar and ukulele chord diagrams you can hear, strummed or as a block chord. 82 chords free, all 302 with Pro.

Reference Tones

Play the exact pitch of any string to tune by ear or train your ear.

Built for iPhone & iPad

Layouts for every iPhone and for iPad in portrait and landscape, with detected and target frequencies always in view.

Accessible, in English & Thai

VoiceOver labels, layouts that hold up at the largest text sizes, and Reduce Motion support. Fully translated into Thai.

Tuner-G Pro

One purchase, no subscription. Any tip in the tip jar unlocks Pro too.

Strobe Display

A strobe view for the finest adjustments.

Frequency Spectrum

See the live spectrum of what you play.

Full Chord Library

All 302 guitar and ukulele chords.

Sweetened Tunings

Intonation-compensated tunings that sound better.

Custom Tunings

Create, save, export and share your own tunings.

High Precision & No Ads

Cents shown to 0.1, and every ad removed.
